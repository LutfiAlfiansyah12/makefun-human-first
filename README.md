An invitation for curious people to pick a coding bounty, build a real project with their AI agent, and share the finished work. The ad presents a simple path: choose a challenge, work with an agent, test the result, and submit the recorded build for a chance at its listed reward. Bounty rules, eligibility, deadlines, and awards vary by challenge; winning is not guaranteed.

node serve.mjs
